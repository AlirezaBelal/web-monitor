WHAT AN ALERT MEANS

A message example is not a notification.

Change detected

A normalized content snapshot differs from the previous successful check. First observations establish a baseline and do not alert.

Retry exhausted

A simulated failure stays visible after two retries. A later successful check can return the target to a healthy state.

Delivery boundary

Production notification channels are not connected here. The log is an illustration; it is not sent to email, SMS, or chat.

No URLs are fetched, no cookies or credentials are used, and no monitoring schedule persists after the page closes.
